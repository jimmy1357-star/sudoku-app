Thermometer Rules

Thermometer Constraint:

Numbers along a thermometer must strictly increase moving from the circular bulb end toward the flat tip.

Example: A 3-cell thermometer starting at a bulb could contain 1 ➔ 4 ➔ 8, but not 2 ➔ 2 ➔ 5 or 6 ➔ 3 ➔ 9.
